- Use `imageRefId` as the stable local key in mock story data.
- Treat `sourcePage` as the canonical reference URL; do not assume third-party hotlinking or redistribution rights.
- Keep cover references behind an asset adapter so a future migration does not change story contracts.

## Content Boundary

- These records represent public Library snapshots only.
- They do not model Studio drafts, canon entities, generation prompts, moderation queues, or private author workspace state.
- A future production adapter should replace mock records with immutable published snapshots after moderation and publication checks.
- Reviews, follows, reader history, and comments remain UI mock states until their backend contracts are explicitly defined.
